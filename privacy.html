Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy covers shorts-uploader (also registered as the app Kinetic Toon Poster), a private tool used only by the owner of Kinetic Toon to publish their own animated videos to their own YouTube channel, Instagram account, Facebook Page and TikTok account, and to read the performance of those videos.

YouTube API Services

shorts-uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Your use of YouTube data is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

What data is accessed

YouTube (Google OAuth scopes youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly): the tool uploads video files, titles, descriptions and tags that the user created to the user's own channel; reads the channel name and the list of the channel's own videos; and reads aggregate performance figures of the channel's own videos (views, average percentage viewed, likes, shares, comments and subscribers gained per video, and the audience retention curve).

Instagram and Facebook (Meta permissions instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, business_management): the tool publishes the same videos as Reels on the owner's Instagram account and Facebook Page, and reads the IDs and links of those posts.

TikTok (TikTok scopes user.info.basic, video.upload): the tool reads the basic profile of the connected account (display name, avatar, open ID) to confirm which account is connected, and sends the owner's videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts. The owner reviews and posts each draft in the TikTok app.

The tool never accesses other people's accounts, viewers' personal data, private messages, comments' contents, followers lists or any data of other channels or pages.

How data is used, stored and shared

  • All sign-in tokens (Google, Meta, TikTok) are stored only in local files on the owner's own computer and are used only for the actions above.
  • A local log on the same computer keeps the IDs, titles, links and publish times of the published videos, to avoid duplicate posts.
  • Performance figures are used only to learn which kinds of videos work best (for example which topics or openings keep viewers watching). Raw figures are deleted within 30 days; only general conclusions are kept.
  • To let Instagram download a video, the tool briefly serves that single video file over a temporary encrypted link (Cloudflare) for about one minute; the link then stops working.
  • No data is sent anywhere except to the platform it belongs to (Google, Meta, TikTok) and that temporary link. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The tool uses no cookies and no tracking. This website uses no cookies or analytics.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Meta (Instagram / Facebook): Facebook Settings → Business integrations, or Instagram Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Deleting the local token files removes all stored access immediately. The local logs can be deleted at any time.
  • Stored platform data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. To request deletion or ask a question, contact toonkinetic@gmail.com; requests are handled within 7 days.
